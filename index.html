Repeat computer tasks (RPA)

If you or your staff click through the same screens every day, a bot can do those clicks for you. This is called RPA, short for robotic process automation. It works with the programs you already have, even the old ones.

For example
Every morning someone copies new orders from the website into the shipping program, one at a time. A bot does it before you get in and leaves a short note of what it moved
How we start

You stay in charge

How the safety works

You stay in charge. Always.

A lot of owners are nervous about AI right now, and that makes sense. So every ChadBot setup follows the same four rules.

You approve first

Nothing gets sent, paid or deleted until you say yes. ChadBot gets it ready and waits for you.

Your data stays home

It runs on a computer in your office. Your files and customer lists stay on your machines. If a step ever needs an outside service, I tell you exactly what goes out before we turn it on.

Everything is logged

You get a plain-English list of what it did and when. No guessing.

Easy off switch

One click stops it. You never need me to turn it off.
